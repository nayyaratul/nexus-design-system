# RatingStar

A single star glyph with three visual states: `filled`, `empty`, and `half`. Compose multiple instances to build a full rating display.

## Half star

The half state layers a clipped filled star over an empty star to achieve the partial-fill effect.

<Canvas of={RatingStarStories.Half} />

## Sizes

Five sizes from `xs` to `xl` for different contexts.

<Canvas of={RatingStarStories.Sizes} />

## Disabled

Disabled stars are visually muted and typically used for read-only ratings.

<Canvas of={RatingStarStories.Disabled} />

## Accessibility

- Each star is marked `aria-hidden` since it is a decorative glyph. The parent rating component should provide an accessible label (e.g., "4 out of 5 stars").
