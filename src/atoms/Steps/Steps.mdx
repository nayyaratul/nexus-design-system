# Steps

A progress stepper that displays a sequence of numbered or icon-based steps with connectors. Each step automatically derives its status (complete, active, or upcoming) from the `activeStep` index. Compose with `Steps` and `StepItem`.

<Primary />

<Controls />

## All Complete

When `activeStep` exceeds the number of steps, every step shows as complete with a checkmark icon.

<Canvas of={StepsStories.AllComplete} />

## With Icons

Pass a Lucide icon component to the `icon` prop on each `StepItem` to replace the default step number. Completed steps still show a checkmark.

<Canvas of={StepsStories.WithIcons} />

## Vertical Orientation

Use `orientation="vertical"` for sidebar wizards or order-tracking UIs where vertical space is more appropriate.

<Canvas of={StepsStories.Vertical} />

## Sizes

Three sizes are available: `sm`, `md` (default), and `lg`. The marker, icon, and connector scale accordingly.

<Canvas of={StepsStories.Sizes} />

## With Content

In vertical orientation, each `StepItem` can accept children for additional body content beneath the label and description.

<Canvas of={StepsStories.WithContent} />

## Accessibility

- The container uses `role="list"` and each step uses `role="listitem"`.
- The active step is marked with `aria-current="step"`.
- Step status (complete, active, upcoming) is communicated visually through colour and icon; pair with descriptive labels for screen reader clarity.
