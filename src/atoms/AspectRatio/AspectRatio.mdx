# AspectRatio

Constrains child content to a specific width-to-height ratio. Built on Radix UI's AspectRatio primitive. Use it to maintain consistent proportions for images, videos, and placeholder areas across responsive layouts.

<Primary />

<Controls />

## 16 : 9

The standard widescreen ratio, ideal for video embeds and hero images.

<Canvas of={AspectRatioStories.SixteenNine} />

## Square (1 : 1)

A square aspect ratio, commonly used for avatars and thumbnail grids.

<Canvas of={AspectRatioStories.Square} />

## Portrait (9 : 16)

A tall portrait ratio for mobile-first media and vertical card layouts.

<Canvas of={AspectRatioStories.NineSixteen} />

## With Image

Pass any content as children. Images should use `object-fit: cover` to fill the container without distortion.

<Canvas of={AspectRatioStories.WithImage} />

## Accessibility

- This component is purely presentational and does not introduce any ARIA roles.
- Ensure images inside the container have appropriate `alt` text.
