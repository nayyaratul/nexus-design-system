Rule for 14px button text (normal weight): AA requires ≥ 4.5:1. For each scale, the minimum step that passes on white is Step 50 (Grey/Red/Green/Blue) or Step 50 (Yellow). On dark (#080808), it's Step 40 for chromatic scales and Step 40–50 for greys. Steps marked ⚠ clear 4.5:1 only marginally — prefer one step darker (higher step number on white; lower on dark) for robust compliance across display variability.

Yellow exception: Yellow 20–30 appear bright but sit at low contrast on white. Always use Yellow 50+ for text on white. Yellow is best used as a background tint with dark (Step 70+) text on top.
